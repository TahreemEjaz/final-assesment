Eligibility Criteria

Applicant must

  • domicile from any district of the Punjab
  • passed intermediate/equivalent examination
  • The candidate must not be older than 21 years of age
  • Students cleared intermediate / A-Levels in the last calendar year.
  • a placement in a university listed in the top 100 QS World General Ranking and top 100 in QS Subject-wise Ranking.

Applicants Not Eligible

  • The candidate who is already availing any other international scholarship or studying abroad
  • The candidates who are already availing any other, HEC/Government Scholarship for Bachelors are not eligible to apply.
  • The candidate has dual nationality
